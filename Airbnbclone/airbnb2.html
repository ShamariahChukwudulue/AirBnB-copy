About Hotel Lorette - Astotel

The 3* Hotel Lorette welcomes you to the 9th arrondissement, between Montmartre, Opéra Garnier and the Grands Boulevards. Housed in an authentic renovated Haussmann building, our team of professionals is here to make your stay unforgettable, whether you're in Paris for business or pleasure. As with all Astotel hotels, end your days on a high note with complimentary beverages and sweet and savory treats in the hotel lobby, served every afternoon until late in the evening.
